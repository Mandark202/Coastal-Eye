About Us

Data, empathy, and action—working together for safer coasts.

CoastalEye leverages AI and machine learning to analyze sensor networks, satellite feeds, and historical records. We detect subtle anomalies indicating threats such as rising sea levels, cyclonic activity, harmful algal blooms, and more.

We deliver timely, actionable alerts to authorities and communities, encouraging transparent collaboration and proactive safety.
